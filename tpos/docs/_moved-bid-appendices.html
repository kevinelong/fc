Appendix J · MVP Scope Matrix & Release Roadmap

The specification describes the full product. To bid accurately, the work must be cut into releases — otherwise a bidder prices “everything at once,” which is neither costable nor advisable. This appendix proposes a Phase-1 (MVP) cutline and tags every capability MoSCoW. PROPOSAL The cutline is the client’s to confirm; it is the single largest driver of the bid.

Proposed three-phase roadmap. Phase 1 delivers the core sell-loop as a hosted product; Phase 2 adds per-role depth; Phase 3 opens new markets and delivery models. Self-host, the platform-admin billing plane, additional accounting systems and a mobile field app are deliberately deferred — each is a major cost lever that should not inflate the first bid.

Appendix N · Delivery Stages (Internal Demo → GA)

A maturity ladder, orthogonal to the feature-depth roadmap (Appendix J): each stage takes a slice of the Phase-1 scope and raises the bar on what is real — data, persistence, security, money, SLA. The decisive boundary is between B and C. Stages A–B are facade/prototype-grade (and A is largely already built); C–E are the real engineering priced in Appendix M. The governing rule: data sensitivity gates the quality bar, not the calendar.

N.1 Effort & the two principles

  • A/B are facade; C/D/E are the build. A is essentially done; B is ~1–3 pw of polish on the existing prototype. C–E together are the full ≈95–175 pw Appendix-M estimate, split roughly: C ≈ foundation + core MUST roles (~40–60% of MVP), D ≈ the remainder + security/QA/pen-test packages, E ≈ the UAT/hardening/launch-readiness tail + billing/support setup. Do not let the polished sales demo imply the system is “almost done.”
  • Data sensitivity gates the bar, not time. Tenant isolation is required the moment real data exists (C). Real PII and money — and therefore the legally-required pen test and GLBA Safeguards baseline (Appendix E) — only enter at beta (D), the stage not to rush. SLA, runbooks, billing and support gate GA (E).
