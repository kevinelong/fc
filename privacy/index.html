Privacy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

The short version. You can use codeonline — write code, play games, save to your device — without an account and without giving us any personal information. There are no ads and no trackers. We never sell or share your data. Signing in is optional and only unlocks cloud saves and publishing.

Using it without an account

  • Your programs run locally in your browser (or in the offline app). We don’t need or receive your code to run it.
  • Games you make are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • No sign-in, no email, no personal data is required to learn, build, or play.

If you choose to sign in

Sign-in (Google, GitHub, or email code) is optional. It only exists so you can save your games to the cloud, pick a nickname, and publish games to the community gallery. If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address — to sign you in and send a one-time verification code.
  • A nickname you choose (or one we generate).
  • Your saved games and any games you publish to the community gallery.

That’s all. We don’t build advertising profiles, and we don’t use third-party analytics.

Multiplayer

When you play a multiplayer game, your position and game state are relayed in real time to the other players in your room so the game works. This is not stored — it passes through and is gone.

Children

  • No personal information is required for a child to use codeonline — it works fully without an account.
  • The community gallery (publishing) requires signing in, so it isn’t intended for young children without a guardian.
  • For classrooms: an optional, no-student-account “class code” mode can let students join with a class code and display name; no student email is requested. A teacher (the only account) creates the class. If enabled, the service stores the class code and name, chosen display names, join/activity times, assigned games, and submitted game titles and code. Display names and submitted work can contain personal information, so students should use a generated alias rather than a real name. Classes idle for 90 days are designed to be deleted automatically, and a teacher can delete a class or submission. Classroom mode remains disabled by default while its launch and privacy review are completed. See the teachers page.

Your control

  • You can delete any game you published from the launcher (the × on your community tiles).
  • To delete your account and associated data (email, nickname, saves), contact us and we’ll remove it.
  • Signed out or offline, everything stays on your device.
